## **Brain Tumor**

![Brain Tumor](/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2-1.png)

**A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells in the brain.** Tumors can be benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous). Brain tumors can cause symptoms such as headaches, seizures, or changes in memory and thinking.

Due to the brain’s complexity and vital role, it’s crucial to diagnose any suspected tumor as soon as possible. Treatment will depend on factors like the type, size, and location of the tumor, and may involve surgery, radiation therapy, or chemotherapy to remove or shrink the tumor and manage symptoms.

### **Why Early Diagnosis Matters?**

Early detection improves treatment outcomes. At first, brain tumors might not show obvious symptoms, making regular screenings and consulting with a **neurosurgeon** vital, especially for those at higher risk.
