## **Fortis Institute of Minimally Invasive Brain Spine Surgery Inaugurated at The First Annual Neurosciences Conclave in Mumbai**

![Poor Sleep Can Affect Your Brain](/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/35052_fortis230326-1.jpeg)

The field of neurosciences is advancing rapidly with a strong focus on innovation and patient-centred care. A major step in this direction was the inauguration of a dedicated institute for minimally invasive brain and spine surgery in Mumbai on 21st and 22nd March 2026, during the first annual neurosciences conclave. The event brought together leading clinicians and healthcare experts to discuss the latest advancements in neurology and neurosurgery.

The institute will be led by Dr. Jayesh Sardhara, Director of Minimally Invasive Brain and Spine Surgery, along with the participation of key healthcare leaders, including Dr. Bishnu Panighari, Dr. S. Narayani, and Dr. Vishal Beri. This initiative highlights a continued commitment to improving patient outcomes through advanced, precise, and minimally invasive techniques.

### **A Step Forward in Specialised Neuro Care**

The newly established institute has been designed to address a wide range of neurological and spine-related conditions. By bringing together expertise in brain, spine, and nerve disorders under one platform, it aims to provide comprehensive and coordinated care for patients.

Minimally invasive approaches are at the core of this advancement. These techniques focus on performing complex procedures through smaller incisions, reducing trauma to surrounding tissues and enabling quicker recovery compared to traditional surgical methods.

### **Focus on Innovation and Clinical Excellence**

The launch coincided with a major neurosciences conclave that brought together clinicians, surgeons, and healthcare professionals from across the country. The event served as a platform to discuss the latest developments in neurology, neurosurgery, and spine care.

Key areas of discussion included advancements in stroke management, brain tumour surgery, and modern spine surgery techniques. The exchange of knowledge and clinical experience plays a vital role in improving treatment strategies and ensuring better patient outcomes.

### **The Role of Minimally Invasive Surgery**

Minimally invasive brain and spine surgery represents a shift towards more precise and patient-friendly treatment options. These procedures are designed to minimise disruption to healthy tissues while effectively treating the underlying condition.

### **Key advantages include:**

- **Smaller incisions and reduced tissue damage**\ \ These techniques allow surgeons to access affected areas with minimal disruption, which can significantly reduce post-operative discomfort.
- **Faster recovery and shorter hospital stays**\ \ Patients often experience quicker recovery times, enabling them to return to their daily routines sooner.
- **Lower risk of complications**\ \ Reduced surgical trauma can decrease the likelihood of infection and other complications.
- **Improved surgical precision**\ \ Advanced technology and imaging support more accurate diagnosis and treatment.

### **Importance of Collaborative Learning in Neurosciences**

Events such as neurosciences conclaves play a crucial role in advancing medical knowledge. By encouraging collaboration between specialists in neurology, neurosurgery, and related fields, these platforms help address real-world clinical challenges.

Such initiatives also promote the adoption of evidence-based practices, ensuring that patients receive care that is aligned with the latest global standards.

### **A Patient-Centred Approach to Neurological Care**

Modern neurological care is no longer limited to treating symptoms alone. It focuses on improving overall quality of life, reducing recovery time, and providing personalised treatment plans.

The integration of advanced surgical techniques with multidisciplinary care ensures that patients benefit from a holistic approach-right from diagnosis to rehabilitation.
