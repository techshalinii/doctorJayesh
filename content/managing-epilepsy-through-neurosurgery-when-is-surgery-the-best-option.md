## **Managing Epilepsy Through Neurosurgery: When Is Surgery the Best Option?**

![Stroke Paralysis Recovery and Surgery Guide](/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/3.png)

Epilepsy affects millions of people around the world, and for many, medications successfully control seizures. However, a significant number of individuals continue to experience seizures despite trying multiple treatments. For such cases,

epilepsy surgery

can provide a chance at long-term seizure relief and a better quality of life. With advancements in technology and experienced

neurosurgeons in Mumbai

, surgical options have become safer, more precise, and minimally invasive.

### **Understanding Epilepsy Surgery**

Epilepsy surgery refers to procedures that remove or modify the part of the brain responsible for triggering seizures. The goal is

seizure control

without affecting vital functions like speech or movement. Common surgical approaches include:

- ✓ **Temporal lobectomy** for temporal lobe epilepsy, the most common type
- ✓ Removal of **brain lesions** when tumors or scars cause seizures
- ✓ **Minimally invasive epilepsy surgery** using laser ablation or targeted treatment techniques

These surgeries aim to significantly reduce or completely stop

drug-resistant seizures.

### **Why Surgery May Be Necessary**

Medications remain the first line of treatment, but if two or more anti-seizure medicines fail to work effectively, the condition is considered

medically refractory

or drug-resistant. Ongoing seizures can severely affect daily life, increasing risks of injuries, cognitive decline, and psychological stress. Surgery is recommended when:

- ✓ The seizure focus is clearly identified.
- ✓ The affected area can be safely removed.
- ✓ Seizures continue despite proper medical therapy

A thorough evaluation from a neurosurgeon consultation is crucial to make the right decision.

### Who Qualifies for Epilepsy Surgery?

Patients may be candidates if they have:

- Persistent seizures that do not respond to medications
- Epilepsy linked to a specific lesion, like a tumor, scar, or malformation
- Seizures originating from an area that does not control critical functions
- A positive response in brain mapping tests and neurological assessments

Diagnostic tools such as EEG, MRI, PET scans, and neuropsychological tests help specialists determine suitability.

### How Effective Is Epilepsy Surgery?

Research has shown that many individuals achieve a major improvement in seizure control after surgery. For temporal lobe epilepsy, success rates are especially high, with a large percentage of patients becoming seizure-free or experiencing only rare episodes. The results contribute to better independence, academic or work performance, and overall well-being. Recovery varies by procedure, but most patients return to normal activities once their neurosurgeon approves.

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

- **Q: Who qualifies for epilepsy surgery?**\ Patients who continue to have seizures despite trying at least two appropriate anti-seizure medications may be evaluated for surgery. Detailed tests are performed to identify the exact source of seizures and check if it can be treated safely.
- **Q: What is the success rate of epilepsy surgery in seizure control?**\ The success rate depends on the type of epilepsy and surgery. Many patients, especially those undergoing temporal lobectomy, achieve complete seizure freedom or significant improvement. Early diagnosis and expert treatment help maximize results.

### **Conclusion**

Epilepsy surgery has changed the lives of people who once struggled with uncontrollable seizures. By combining advanced technology with skilled care in

neurological treatment in Mumbai

, patients now have a real opportunity to regain control and live more confidently. A consultation with an experienced

neurosurgeon in Mumbai

can help determine whether surgery is the most effective path forward.
