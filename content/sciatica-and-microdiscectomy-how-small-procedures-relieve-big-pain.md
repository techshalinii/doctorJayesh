## **Sciatica and Microdiscectomy: How Small Procedures Relieve Big Pain**

![Stroke Paralysis Recovery and Surgery Guide](/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/6.png)

Sciatica can feel like an electric shock shooting from the lower back down the leg, making everyday activities difficult. When pain continues despite medicines, physiotherapy, or injections, microdiscectomy surgery becomes a powerful solution. This minimally invasive procedure targets the root cause of sciatica to restore mobility and comfort. People in Mumbai dealing with persistent nerve pain often turn to microdiscectomy surgery because it offers fast relief and a quicker return to routine life.

### **What Causes Sciatica?**

Sciatica usually occurs when a herniated lumbar disc presses on a spinal nerve. This most commonly affects the L4-L5 or L5-S1 levels in the lower back. The compressed nerve results in pain, numbness, or tingling down the leg.

### **What Is Microdiscectomy Surgery?**

Microdiscectomy is a minimally invasive spine surgery where a neurosurgeon removes the part of the herniated disc pressing on the affected nerve root. Through a small incision and advanced tools, the surgeon decompresses the nerve without disturbing healthy tissues. This makes recovery much smoother compared to conventional open spine surgery.

#### **Benefits of Microdiscectomy**

- ✓ Relief from leg pain caused by nerve compression
- ✓ Quick improvement in mobility
- ✓ Small incision with minimal scarring
- ✓ Lower risk of complications
- ✓ Shorter hospital stay
- ✓ Faster return to work or daily activities

Patients seeking sciatica treatment in Mumbai often choose this approach for its effectiveness and modern surgical precision.

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

- **Q: When is microdiscectomy recommended for sciatica?**\ Microdiscectomy is advised when:\  1. Pain persists for more than six to eight weeks despite conservative treatment\  2. Symptoms include significant leg pain, numbness, or muscle weakness\  3. MRI confirms a disc herniation compressing a spinal nerve\  4. The patient experiences difficulty standing or walking due to pain\  A spinal neurosurgeon evaluates each case to determine whether L4-L5 surgery or another level needs decompression.
- **Q: How long is the recovery after microdiscectomy?** \ Most patients notice pain relief immediately after the surgery. They can usually:\  1. Walk on the same day\  2. Return home within 24 to 48 hours\  3. Resume office work in two to four weeks\  4. Restart physical exercises gradually with guidance\  Full recovery varies by age, overall health, and adherence to physiotherapy. Avoiding heavy lifting in the early recovery phase is important for the best long-term results.

### **Conclusion**

Sciatica caused by a herniated disc can severely limit quality of life, but microdiscectomy offers a proven and efficient path to relief. With precise spinal nerve decompression and faster recovery, this minimally invasive spine surgery helps patients return to a pain-free and active life. Consulting an experienced spinal neurosurgeon ensures the right treatment plan for lasting comfort and mobility.
