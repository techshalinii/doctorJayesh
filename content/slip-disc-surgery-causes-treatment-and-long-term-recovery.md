## **Slip Disc Surgery: Causes, Treatment, and Long-Term Recoveryy**

![Slip Disc Surgery Treatment](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Untitled-design-2.png)

Herniated Disc Treatment and Recovery Guide

A slipped disc, also called a herniated or prolapsed disc, is a common cause of back and leg pain. Many patients improve with medication and physical therapy; however, some may need surgery if their symptoms continue or get worse. With modern spine surgery techniques available in Mumbai, most patients find significant pain relief and better mobility

.

### **What Is a Slipped Disc?**

A slipped disc occurs when the soft inner portion of a spinal disc bulges or protrudes through its outer covering. This can compress nearby nerves and cause symptoms such as:

- ✓ Severe **lower back pains**
- ✓ Pain radiating to the **leg (sciatica)** ✓ **Numbness** or **tingling**  ✓ Muscle **weakness**  ✓ Difficulty **walking** or **standing** for long periods

Lumbar disc herniation is more common, but it can also occur in the neck (cervical spine).

### **When Is Slip Disc Surgery Needed?**

Most cases improve with conservative treatment. However, surgery may be recommended if:

- ✓ Pain persists despite 6–8 weeks of treatment
- ✓ There is progressive muscle weakness
- ✓ Severe nerve compression is present
- ✓ Loss of bladder or bowel control occurs (medical emergency)
- ✓ MRI confirms significant disc prolapse

Early evaluation by a neurospine specialist in Mumbai helps determine the right treatment plan.

### **Types of Surgery for Herniated Disc**

Modern spine surgery focuses on minimally invasive techniques:

- ✓ **Microdiscectomy** Removal of the herniated disc fragment using a small incision and microscope.
- ✓ **Endoscopic spine surgeryy** A minimally invasive approach using a tiny camera and instruments.
- ✓ **Laminectomy (if needed)y** Removal of a small portion of bone to relieve nerve pressure.

These procedures aim to relieve nerve compression while preserving spinal stability.

### Recovery After Slipped Disc Surgery

Recovery is usually quick with modern techniques. Most patients:

- ✓ Walk within 24 hours
- ✓ Return home in 1–3 days
- ✓ Resume light activities in 2–4 weeks
- ✓ Return to a normal routine within 4–8 weeks

Physiotherapy and posture correction play an important role in long-term success.

### Long-Term Outcomes

Slip disc surgery has a high success rate when performed for the right indications. Long-term results depend on:

- ✓ Duration of nerve compression
- ✓ Overall spine health
- ✓ Weight management and lifestyle
- ✓ Adherence to rehabilitation exercises

With proper care and follow-up, patients treated for a slipped disc in Mumbai often return to pain-free, active lives.

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

- **Q: Is slipped disc surgery safe?** \ Yes, when done by a skilled spine surgeon, minimally invasive procedures are safe and effective with low complication rates.
- **Q: Can a slipped disc come back after surgery?** \ Recurrence is rare, but it can happen. Keeping good posture, exercising regularly, and controlling weight can lower the risk.

### Conclusion

Slip disc surgery provides long-lasting relief when other treatments don't work. With modern minimally invasive spine methods and skilled neurospine care in Mumbai, patients can regain comfort, mobility, and quality of life. Early consultation helps ensure timely treatment and faster recovery.
